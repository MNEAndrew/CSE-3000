## Personal Statement on Ethics

My view on ethics after reading chapter 5 of Ethical Machines, is that transparency is one of the most important responsibilities in AI. People should be able to understand when AI is being used, what it is being used for, and just as importantly, how it is not being used or where its use is forbidden.
I also believe ethical AI is not just about creating powerful tools, but about setting clear boundaries. There should be open communication about the purpose of an AI system, its limits, and the decisions it should not be allowed to make on its own. This would help protect users, supports fairness, and makes it possible for society to decide what kinds of AI use are acceptable.
